# Comparison2 — editorial alternative

Routes: `/comparison2/` and `/comparison2/<competitor>/` open the English version. Canonical locale pairs are `/en/comparison2/` and `/uk/comparison2/`, with nine individual comparisons in each language. The original `/en/compare/` and `/uk/compare/` pages remain unchanged. Neither section is added to the header or footer menus.

This version describes each capability in prose: the competitor's actual workflow, how Briller fits, and a specific audience's decision with a contextual link to a localized solution page. All 35 capability sections have their own audience example. Capability tables and review-method disclaimers are omitted. Specific uncertainties appear under “To confirm” / “Уточнити”, including unresolved pricing, allowance, export and licence details. An official competitor link with its logo is retained at the bottom of each article; the homepage screenshot also links to the official site.

Every individual comparison includes Pros, Cons and Best for sections before pricing, with direct links in its contents. These sections reuse the competitor-specific bilingual copy in `roundup.json`, so the guide and detail pages stay consistent. Pros and Cons sit side by side on desktop and stack on mobile; Best for follows as a highlighted paragraph. The direct `/comparison2/<competitor>/` aliases include the same sections.

`articles.json` owns the bilingual capability copy, audience perspectives, solution crosslinks and general confirmation questions. `pricing.json` retains the full tier research, including billing units, sources and explicit Briller plan pairings. Public tables select monthly subscription tiers only and show monthly prices, monthly allowances and the matched Briller plan. Annual offers, free tiers, weekly offers, enterprise quotes and one-time packs are not table rows. `monthlyAllowance`, `monthlyNotes` and `monthlyChecks` keep the displayed copy specific to monthly billing. RoomGPT has no monthly subscription, so its pricing section uses prose. Homestyler's advertised starting rates have an unconfirmed term; its plan names and allowances appear with a monthly-price confirmation label. Never use those starting rates as month-to-month prices. The mobile view stacks the two packages under the same use case. Numeric Briller prices and entitlements stay derived from `content/plans.json`. See `pricing-research-2026-11-02.md` for findings and source conflicts. The original comparison catalogue is retained as its own snapshot.

The pages have canonical/hreflang pairs, social metadata and breadcrumbs. The overview targets searches for best AI interior design tools in 2026 with a matching H1, title, description and short introduction, plus visible buyer questions. Its CollectionPage / ItemList, Article and FAQPage structured data use the actual page content and publication/update dates; no aggregate ratings or unsupported testing claims are added. FAQ markup does not imply eligibility for a Google rich result. The EN/UK overview is indexable in production and listed in sitemap / llms.txt. Individual comparison2 pages stay noindex and outside those listings while their original comparison URLs retain their SEO setup. All previews remain noindex. Pages stay outside the navigation menus and are available by direct URL in local previews and normal static builds.

Validation: normal preview build, TypeScript check, existing comparison tests and `npm run test:comparison2`. Check desktop/mobile prose, TOC links, pricing-table scrolling, confirmation items, direct root routes and locale switching before publishing preview.

Feature illustrations are locally stored WebP screenshots of the competitors' official demonstrations, placed after the relevant competitor paragraph. Captions and alt text are bilingual; each screenshot opens at full size without loading the competitor's site or scripts. The existing branded official-site link at the bottom stays unchanged. `screenshots.json` records the source page, capture date, target element, original dimensions and SHA-256. All nine competitors have feature illustrations. Video demonstrations are captured as still frames with the seek position recorded; the website loads no remote videos or competitor scripts. Do not substitute a broken-image capture or a fabricated interface. Raw PNG captures and capture logs are retained outside Git in `/workspace/exports/comparison2-screenshots-2026-11-03/`.

`brands.json` owns a homepage screenshot and an official wordmark for every competitor. Article heroes pair the introduction with the locally stored homepage capture; the first-fold image loads eagerly. Homepage screenshots in both the overview and individual comparisons link directly to the competitor’s official website in a new tab, with a localized accessible name and no visible caption or full-size link beneath them. Feature demonstration screenshots retain their descriptive captions and full-size image links. The external official-site link at the bottom contains the matching wordmark. Logos keep their original colours and proportions, with a dark well where needed for a light wordmark. Source URLs, capture dates and hashes are recorded for both assets. REimagineHome and Interior AI use their actual rendered wordmarks because their branding is text/CSS; Planner 5D and Foyr use the rendered official SVG. No remote site is embedded. Homepage capture scripts and originals are in `/workspace/exports/comparison2-homepages-2026-11-03/`.

The overview is a long-form buyer's guide using `roundup.json`: a short introduction, quick audience links, contents, nine feature summaries with Pros / Cons / Best for, the monthly-only pricing overview, and a Briller conclusion. Each entry reuses the official homepage screenshot and logo, and links to its detailed comparison. The reference article informed the structure; its copy, rankings, speed claims and older prices are not reused. `reviews.json` accepts only verified Capterra feedback with an author, review date, score, localized paraphrase and source URL. Source-access status is internal; unverified feedback is never displayed or represented by fabricated quotes or ratings. Six review records across four platforms were checked on 2026-11-04: 2026 feedback for Planner 5D and Homestyler, a 2025 Planner 5D billing report, and explicitly dated 2024 examples for HomeDesignsAI and Foyr Neo. See `reviews-research-2026-11-04.md` for attribution, coverage and source notes.
